# PSX Forest Free

**15 free PS1-style pieces for a campsite in the woods**: two pines, an oak and a dead tree whose crowns sway in the wind, a bush, grass and a fern to walk through, a boulder, a stump and a log, a tent, a campfire whose flames burn, split-rail fence, and a grass tile and a dirt path tile, 4 m each, that meet any way round. Low-poly and point-filtered, a scene per piece, ready to drop in. Free for commercial use.

### Want more?

**[PSX Forest](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-forest)** has 87 pieces: 15 trees (pines, spruce,
oaks, a birch, the dead, burnt and fallen), bushes, reeds, flowers and mushrooms, boulders and 4 m
cliff faces, a log cabin to walk into, a well whose crank draws the bucket, a campsite, and ground
tiles whose path turns, ends and crosses, with a night clearing to walk and a Unity package. Same
addon, same look: it installs over this one. The night sky over the camp is one of
[PSX Skyboxes](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-skyboxes)' 26.

### What's inside

- **Trees:** a pine, a small one, an oak and a dead tree, their crowns swaying from a third of the
  way up the trunk.
- **Undergrowth:** a bush, and grass and a fern to walk through.
- **Rock and wood:** a boulder, a sawn stump and a log.
- **The camp:** a tent, a campfire whose flames burn, and a length of split-rail fence.
- **Ground:** a 4 by 4 m grass tile and a dirt path tile, to lay on a 4 m grid.
- **5 animations:** the four trees sway and the fire burns, looping. The moving parts are separate
  objects, so your code can drive them too.
- **15 icons**, 256x256 PNGs with transparency, rendered from the models.
- 24 to 432 triangles a piece, one 1024x1024 texture atlas, shading baked into the vertex colours,
  real-world scale.

### Godot 4.3 to 4.7

An addon with a scene per piece: box colliders on the trees, the bush, the rock, the wood and the
camp, none on the grass and the fern, so the player walks through them; an AnimationPlayer where it
moves, one lit material so your torch falls on them, and a gallery. Tested on Godot 4.3 and 4.7,
on Forward+, Mobile and Compatibility.

```gdscript
# campfire: the piece in your level
campfire.get_node("AnimationPlayer").play("burn")
```

For the full PS1 look, [PSX Look](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-look) turns the pieces (and your
level) over in one line: wobbling vertices, warping textures, 240p, dither and 15-bit colour. Its
[free sampler](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-look-free) has the vertex snap and affine textures.
